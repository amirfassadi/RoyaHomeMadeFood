# Domestic payments

After reviewing the invoice, customers select Zarinpal or bank transfer (card, Paya or Pol). The order request reserves stock but remains pending until payment is confirmed. Invoice number and numeric payment reference are derived from the same random order code and stored uniquely. The reference is for store reconciliation, not a bank-issued structured deposit identifier.

In Admin → Settings → Bank accounts, add up to ten accounts with an account holder and card number and/or IBAN, activate the desired accounts, and enable bank transfer. Persian digits, spaces and separators are normalized. Settings are stored in PostgreSQL and require no VPS environment changes. Inactive or unconfigured methods cannot be selected at checkout. Active destinations are snapshotted on each order and remain recoverable from tracking even after accounts are edited or disabled. No placeholder destination is shown to customers. Apply database migrations and configure ZARINPAL_MERCHANT_ID, NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL and ZARINPAL_SANDBOX before live use. Do not enable production payments until the real merchant account has been tested.

For bank transfers, the administrator must inspect the receiving bank account, then enter the exact amount in Toman and unique bank transaction reference in the order detail. Customer receipts alone do not confirm funds. A transaction lock and unique reference prevent duplicate confirmation and reuse across orders. Cancelled orders cannot be confirmed. Confirmation records paymentConfirmedAt and recalculates delivery from the saved preparation and shipping durations. Pending delivery dates are withheld from customer receipt and tracking responses. createdAt remains the audit timestamp of the request, not payment acceptance.

Zarinpal confirmation relies on server verification, not browser status. Unknown gateway requests are retained for reconciliation; fresh requests can recover through tracking. Existing demo mode does not accept real funds.

A missing payment configuration rejected before order creation returns an explicit checkoutNotStarted response, allowing the customer to edit and retry. Network errors and uncertain transaction results retain the original idempotency key and locked fields. Payment references are internal, unique per order and searchable alongside the customer mobile in the admin order list; they are not bank-issued deposit identifiers. Never mark a bank transfer paid based only on a customer-provided receipt or reference.
